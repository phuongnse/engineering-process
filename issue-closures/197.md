# [consumer-process][engineering-process][2.4.0][feedback-handoff-guidance] Clarify durable feedback handoff and review waiting states

## Request

### Context

The engineering-process self-consumer reported that adjacent guidance made the durable issue requirement and the missing-access handoff ambiguous, even though the lifecycle test and review schema required a real issue URL.

### Expected outcome

A fresh coordinator can distinguish existing-issue reuse, authorized issue creation, and review-pending manual handoff, including the exact action awaited before shared-process review may submit.

## Evidence and scope

### Evidence

Implementation and guidance changes were merged in PR #206, including the feedback-handoff clarification. The v2.5.0 release recorded the accepted #197-#205 consumer change, and the v2.6.0 adoption PR verified the current self-consumer after adoption.

### Scope

Durable feedback handoff guidance and its existing lifecycle boundary; no automatic issue creation, no weakened recordUrl requirement, and no new feedback ledger.

### Acceptance criteria

The issue-specific guidance is implemented, the existing missing-URL behavior remains enforced, the released behavior is adopted, and consumer verification confirms the resulting process state.
