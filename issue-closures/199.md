# [consumer-process][engineering-process][2.4.0][guidance-authority] Consolidate repeated guidance and correct migration tense

## Request

### Context

The engineering-process self-consumer reported repeated guidance and migration wording that obscured which instructions were authoritative and whether they described current or future behavior.

### Expected outcome

A reader can locate one coherent authoritative explanation of the shared process and read migration instructions in the correct tense without reconciling contradictory copies.

## Evidence and scope

### Evidence

The guidance and migration wording correction was merged in PR #206. The v2.5.0 release recorded the accepted #197-#205 consumer change, and the v2.6.0 adoption PR verified the current self-consumer after adoption.

### Scope

Shared process guidance and migration wording in consumer-facing documentation; no unrelated product behavior, release policy, or new lifecycle.

### Acceptance criteria

Repeated or conflicting guidance is consolidated or corrected, the current adopted process remains internally consistent, and the released behavior is adopted and verified by the consumer.
