# [consumer-process][engineering-process][2.4.0][reviewer-unavailability] Establish a bounded recovery decision for unavailable reviewers

## Request

### Context

The engineering-process self-consumer reported that reviewer unavailability needed a bounded recovery decision that preserved independent-review ownership and did not leave completed lifecycle state ambiguous.

### Expected outcome

A coordinator can identify the bounded recovery action when a reviewer is unavailable, preserve the review assignment history, and reopen completed work when a later update invalidates its evidence.

## Evidence and scope

### Evidence

The reviewer recovery and completed-change reopening correction was merged in PR #206 at commit 39c2f19. The v2.5.0 release recorded the accepted #197-#205 consumer change, and the v2.6.0 adoption PR verified the current self-consumer after adoption.

### Scope

Reviewer assignment recovery, review-context ownership, and evidence invalidation after updates; no reviewer substitution without recorded conflict, no self-review waiver, and no second lifecycle.

### Acceptance criteria

Unavailable-reviewer recovery is bounded and recorded, stale completed evidence reopens correctly, independent review remains required, and the released behavior is adopted and verified by the consumer.
