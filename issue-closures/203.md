# [consumer-process][engineering-process][2.4.0][adoption-verification-scope] Define proportional consumer verification for process adoption

## Request

### Context

The engineering-process self-consumer reported that process adoption needed proportional verification guidance while retaining the consumer-owned required baseline and the full boundary for mixed changes.

### Expected outcome

A consumer can distinguish guidance-only adoption, runtime or schema migration, mixed product changes, and unknown impact, selecting proportional checks without silently omitting required profiles.

## Evidence and scope

### Evidence

The proportional adoption guidance correction was merged in PR #206 at commit 7dd5f44. The v2.5.0 release recorded the accepted #197-#205 consumer change, and the v2.6.0 adoption PR verified the current self-consumer after adoption.

### Scope

Consumer adoption verification scope and guidance; no omitted baseline profiles, no automatic waiver, no consumer CI or branch-protection reconfiguration, and no unrelated product behavior.

### Acceptance criteria

Adoption guidance identifies the proportional boundary and required full checks for mixed or unknown impact, the consumer adopts the released behavior, and consumer verification confirms the result.
